# Season lobby artwork

These are the in-game Fortnite lobby backdrops for every chapter and season,
archived from the Fortnite Wiki lobby timeline:

https://fortnite.weirdgloop.org/w/Lobby

Fortnite and its artwork are trademarks of Epic Games, Inc. VAULTED is an
independent fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Epic Games.

Chapter 1 Seasons 1 and 3 reuse the pre-season backdrop that shipped with those
builds. Chapter 1 Season 2 uses the Winterfest 2017 backdrop. The rest map one-
to-one to the season's default lobby background.
